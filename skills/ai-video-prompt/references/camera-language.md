# Camera & Cinematography Language Reference

Use this vocabulary when building prompts for any video or image platform.

---

## Shot Types

| Shot | Framing | Emotional effect |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| Extreme wide | Full environment, subject tiny | Scale, isolation, establishing |
| Wide | Full body in environment | Context, grounding |
| Medium | Waist up | Conversational, balanced |
| Medium close-up | Chest up | Emotional weight, intimacy |
| Close-up | Face only | Intensity, connection |
| Extreme close-up | Eyes, hands, texture | Raw detail, tension |
| Two-shot | Two people in frame | Relationship, dialogue |
| Over-the-shoulder | Behind one person toward another | POV-adjacent, conversational |
| Low angle | Looking up at subject | Power, dominance |
| High angle | Looking down at subject | Vulnerability, smallness |
| Bird's eye | Directly overhead | Abstract, detached |
| POV / selfie | First-person, camera held by subject | Immersive, UGC, authentic |

---

## Camera Movements

| Movement | What it does | When to use |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Static / locked off | No movement | Tension, focus, stillness |
| Slow pan | Camera rotates horizontally | Scanning, revealing |
| Tilt | Camera rotates vertically | Revealing height, scale |
| Dolly in | Camera moves toward subject | Building intimacy or tension |
| Dolly out | Camera pulls away | Revealing scale, creating distance |
| Tracking shot | Camera follows subject laterally | Energy, momentum, journey |
| Crane / jib | Camera rises or sweeps vertically | Epic reveal, scale |
| Aerial / drone | Overhead or descending | Establishing location, grandeur |
| Handheld | Organic, slightly shaky | Documentary, naturalistic, urgency |
| Steadicam / gimbal | Smooth follow | Cinematic, controlled, polished |
| Whip pan | Fast rotation | Disorientation, transition, energy |
| Dutch angle | Camera tilted on axis | Unease, instability |
| Push-in | Slow dolly forward | Growing realization, emphasis |

## Lens Language

| Lens | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Wide-angle (16-24mm) | Environment dominant, slight edge distortion |
| Normal (35-50mm) | Natural perspective, how the eye sees |
| Portrait (85mm) | Flattering compression, shallow DOF |
| Telephoto (135-200mm) | Compressed depth, subject close to background |
| Macro | Extreme detail, textures revealed |
| Anamorphic | Cinematic horizontal bokeh, lens flares |

### Depth of field
- **Shallow** (f/1.4–f/2.8): Subject isolated, background in soft bokeh
- **Medium** (f/4–f/5.6): Subject sharp, background slightly soft
- **Deep** (f/8–f/16): Everything sharp front to back

## Lighting Setups

### Natural light
| Setup | Mood |
|-------|------|
| Golden hour (sun low, warm) | Warm, romantic, nostalgic |
| Blue hour (just after sunset) | Cool, melancholic, contemplative |
| Overcast / diffused | Soft, even, documentary |
| Hard midday sun | Harsh, raw, confrontational |
| Backlit / rim light | Ethereal, separation, halo effect |
| Dappled (through trees/blinds) | Organic, textured, natural |

### Studio / artificial
| Setup | Mood |
|-------|------|
| Three-point (key + fill + back) | Professional, balanced |
| Single source / chiaroscuro | Dramatic, moody, noir |
| Practical lights (lamps, screens) | Naturalistic, lived-in |
| Neon / colored gels | Stylized, urban, music-video |
| Fluorescent overhead | Clinical, institutional, cold |

## Audio Direction (for platforms that support it)

### Dialogue
Put speech in quotes with attribution:
- `She says, "Nobody teaches you how to take care of your aging parents."`
- `Voiceover: "This is where it all began."`

### Sound effects
Label with SFX:
- `SFX: Footsteps on gravel, slowing to a stop.`
- `SFX: The door creaks open. A beat of silence.`

### Ambient
Describe the soundscape:
- `Ambient: Birdsong. Wind through trees. Distant traffic.`
- `Ambient: The hum of a hospital corridor. Muffled intercom.`
