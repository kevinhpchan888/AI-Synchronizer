
# ebook-distribution

## When to load

- Kevin has final production files (EPUB, PDF, paperback interior, both covers)
- He is deciding where to sell: KDP-exclusive, wide, own-store, or hybrid
- He is preparing launch (pricing, keywords, categories, description, reviews, ads)
- He is using the book as a lead magnet for his Shopify store

## Kevin's distribution decision tree

```
Purpose of the book?
├── Authority/brand-building (B) → Wide distribution, $4.99-$9.99 ebook, KDP paperback
│                                   + own-store PDF, + Klaviyo lead capture tie-in
├── KDP info product (C) → Default KDP Select for 90 days to test KU velocity,
│                           then decide wide at Day 91
└── Both (B+C merged, Kevin's plan) → Hybrid: KDP paperback + KDP Select 90d for ebook,
                                       own-store PDF sold at higher price tier,
                                       Klaviyo tie-in throughout
```

## Step 1: Pick the distribution model

### Option A: KDP Select (Amazon exclusive for ebook, 90-day renewable)

Pros:
- Enroll in Kindle Unlimited (KU) — readers can borrow for free, Kevin earns per page read (~$0.004/page in 2026, verify current rate)
- Access to KDP promotional tools: Kindle Countdown Deals, Free Book Promotion (5 days per 90-day cycle)
- Higher visibility in Kindle Store

Cons:
- Ebook must NOT be available in any other digital store during the 90-day period
- Paperback and direct PDF sales on own store ARE allowed (KDP Select exclusivity is ebook-only)
- If Kevin withdraws early, he cannot re-enroll for several months

### Option B: Wide distribution

Pros:
- List on Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play, B&N Nook, library aggregators
- Tool: **Draft2Digital** (free, takes 10% cut) or **Smashwords** (similar)
- Diversifies away from Amazon platform risk
- Library availability (OverDrive) via D2D

Cons:
- No KU royalties
- Amazon gives wide books slightly less algorithmic love
- Fragmented launch: harder to concentrate reviews

### Recommendation for Kevin (B+C merged)

**Day 0-90: KDP Select + Paperback + own-store PDF.** Learn KU velocity. Concentrate reviews. Use Countdown Deals for launch week.

**Day 91 decision**: If KU payouts > 50% of total ebook revenue, renew KDP Select. If <30%, switch to wide via Draft2Digital.

Paperback always goes through KDP Print (or IngramSpark for bookstore distribution — more on this below). Direct PDF always on Kevin's own Shopify regardless of ebook strategy.

## Step 2: KDP account setup (for Singapore author)

### Tax setup

Kevin is Singapore tax-resident. Amazon KDP withholds 30% US tax by default. With proper forms, withholds 0%.

1. **Create KDP account** at kdp.amazon.com
2. **Tax interview** during setup: complete as "Individual" or "Business" (Pte Ltd is Business)
3. **Form W-8BEN-E** (for Pte Ltd) or **W-8BEN** (for individual SG tax resident):
   - Claim Singapore tax residency
   - Claim treaty benefits under Singapore-US tax treaty (Article on royalties)
   - Singapore-US treaty rate: **5% withholding on book royalties** (not 0%, verify current)
4. **Bank details**: SG bank account or Wise/Airwallex USD account
5. **GST registration**: if Kevin's Pte Ltd is GST-registered, KDP income may affect reporting — route to `dropship-legal-compliance` and `dropship-cashflow-ops`

### Author Central

Separate from KDP: **authorcentral.amazon.com**
- Links all books under Kevin's author page
- Add photo, bio, social links
- Claim books across US/UK/DE/FR/ES/IT/JP/IN marketplaces (each is a separate claim)
- Respond to reviews (sparingly, and never defensively — Amazon can remove the author)

## Step 3: Pricing strategy

### Ebook pricing tiers (and their royalty math)

| Price | Royalty rate | Kevin earns per sale (approx) | Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| $0.99 | 35% | $0.35 | Launch promo only, otherwise signals low quality |
| $2.99 | 70% | $2.05 (after delivery fee) | Sweet-spot floor — jumps to 70% tier |
| $4.99 | 70% | $3.40 | Standard launch price for serious nonfiction |
| $6.99 | 70% | $4.75 | Strong post-launch price for 200+ page book |
| $9.99 | 70% | $6.85 | Upper bound of 70% tier |
| $10.00+ | 35% | varies | Drops back to 35% — avoid unless premium positioning |

**Delivery fee**: Amazon charges $0.15/MB on 70% tier. A typical 40k word ebook is ~0.5 MB, so fee is ~$0.08. Larger files (with many images) cost more.

### Kevin's pricing recommendation (authority book, B+C)

- **Launch week**: $0.99 or $2.99 with Kindle Countdown Deal to stack reviews
- **Standard**: $6.99 for 120-180 page business book (right tier for operator-authored)
- **Paperback**: $14.99-$19.99 (KDP paperback royalty is printed-cost-minus-40%; do the math with KDP's royalty calculator)
- **Direct PDF on Kevin's Shopify**: $19.99-$29.99 (premium positioning, bypasses Amazon cut, lives alongside his physical products)
- **KU reads**: passive income, Kevin doesn't set this

### Why direct PDF is priced higher

- Amazon buyers have price anchors ($6.99 "normal" for ebooks)
- Kevin's store buyers already self-selected into premium positioning
- Direct sale = 100% margin minus Shopify transaction fees (~2.9% + 30c)
- Allows bundling: "Book + 30-min consult $499" or "Book + spreadsheet templates $79"

## Step 4: Categories and keywords (the findability layer)

### Categories

KDP allows **3 categories** (2 via the upload form, 1 via email to KDP Support). Pick:

1. **Broadest category that still fits** — for ranking as #1 New Release
2. **Narrow sub-category** — for bestseller status in a thin category
3. **Adjacent category** — covers readers who browse differently

Example for Kevin (dropshipping/DTC authority):
- Kindle eBooks › Business & Money › Small Business & Entrepreneurship › E-commerce
- Kindle eBooks › Business & Money › Marketing & Sales › Digital Marketing
- Kindle eBooks › Business & Money › Economics › Exports & Imports

Use **Publisher Rocket** (paid, ~$200 one-time, or monthly) or **K-lytics reports** for category research. Cheaper alternative: manual scan of Amazon category dropdowns, find categories where #1 has <50 reviews (winnable).

### Keywords

KDP allows **7 keyword phrases** (up to ~50 characters each). Use ALL 7. Strategy:

1. 2-3 **broad phrases** readers actually type ("dropshipping business", "e-commerce brand building")
2. 2-3 **long-tail phrases** less competitive ("singapore dropshipping operator", "private label transition dropshipping")
3. 1-2 **situational phrases** ("how to scale shopify store", "meta ads ecommerce 2026")

Rules:
- Do NOT repeat words already in title/subtitle (Amazon indexes those separately)
- Do NOT use competitor names, trademarks, or author names (Amazon may remove book)
- Do NOT use "free", "bestseller", or promotional claims

### A+ Content (free, underused)

Author Central → Manage A+ Content. Add:
- 1 enhanced module with author photo + credibility intro (above-the-fold on product page)
- 1 module with book structure preview (TOC teaser)
- 1 module with testimonials / blurbs (once Kevin has them)

A+ content lifts conversion measurably. Takes ~2 hours to set up. Do it before launch.

## Step 5: Book description (the highest-leverage copy on the page)

### Structure (for nonfiction business book)

```
[Hook headline — bold, capital case, 5-10 words. A promise or a question.]

[3-5 sentences of the problem. Reader's pain. Not Kevin's credentials yet.]

[2-3 sentences: why the usual solutions fail. Name the alternatives and dismiss them.]

[Transition: "In [Book Title], Kevin Chan reveals..."]

[Bullet list of 5-7 specific things the reader will learn. Each bullet = one chapter or one big idea.]
• [Specific outcome 1]
• [Specific outcome 2]
...

[Social proof line if available: "Used by 1,000+ operators..." or "Based on $X in ad spend..."]

[CTA: "Get your copy today and..."]
```

### Formatting

- Amazon supports limited HTML in the description box: `<b>`, `<i>`, `<br>`, `<p>`, `<ul>`, `<li>`
- Use `<b>` on the hook headline and the bullet starters
- Break into short paragraphs (mobile reading)
- Max ~4000 characters

### Do not

- Do not use testimonials you don't have yet
- Do not exaggerate ("make $10k a month!!!") — Amazon has an honesty standard and reviews will punish
- Do not use em-dashes (Kevin's rule)

## Step 6: Reviews (the make-or-break at launch)

### Target: 20 reviews in the first 30 days

Under 15 reviews, Amazon's algo barely promotes the book. At 20+, it starts showing it to related-book buyers. At 50+, it gets algorithmic favor in category.

### Legitimate review channels

1. **Launch team** (20-50 people): recruit from Kevin's email list, Twitter, LinkedIn. Give them a free ARC (advance reader copy) 2 weeks before launch. Ask for honest reviews on launch day. Amazon allows this as long as no payment or quid-pro-quo is attached.

2. **BookSprout** ($29/mo): sends ARC to genre readers who commit to reviewing. ~$10-20 per confirmed review.

3. **Pubby** ($15/mo): credit-based review exchange. More common in fiction, but nonfiction works.

4. **NetGalley** ($399-$999 listing fee): expensive but reaches serious reviewers and book bloggers. Recommend skipping for first book.

5. **Readers' Favorite** (free editorial reviews + paid faster tiers): adds "5-star editorial review" to metadata.

### Do NOT

- **Pay for reviews directly.** Against Amazon TOS. Account ban.
- **Swap reviews with another author.** Against Amazon TOS. Account ban.
- **Review your own book or have family review.** Account ban.
- **Use review farms (Fiverr gig selling "50 5-star reviews").** Account ban.

Kevin's long-term brand is worth more than a launch-week shortcut. Refuse these pressures even when Kevin asks.

### Review request flow (in-book and email)

At the end of the book (back matter), a short page:

```
If this book helped you, please leave a quick review on Amazon. Even two sentences matters more than you think — it's what other operators see before they click buy.

[Direct link or QR code to Amazon review page]
```

In Kevin's email flow (Klaviyo): one review-request email 7 days after purchase, one follow-up 21 days after. Nothing more. Do not hassle readers.

## Step 7: Launch plan

### T-minus 6 weeks: file hand-off to KDP

- Upload ebook to KDP as draft (save, do not publish yet)
- Upload paperback draft
- Order paperback proof copy
- Begin Author Central setup
- Apply for ISBN if not via KDP

### T-minus 4 weeks: review team + pre-order

- Activate Kindle ebook pre-order (1-90 days allowed by KDP)
- Recruit launch team (20-50 readers)
- Distribute ARC (via BookFunnel, StoryOrigin, or direct PDF via email)
- Build Klaviyo flow for pre-order buyers ("thanks, review goes live Day 1")

### T-minus 2 weeks: press and outreach

- Podcast outreach (record episodes airing launch week)
- LinkedIn long-form post, Twitter thread previewing key ideas
- Email Kevin's existing list with a "I wrote a book" announcement

### Launch day (Day 0)

- Ebook goes live
- Kindle Countdown Deal at $0.99 for 3 days (stacks early reviews)
- Launch team submits reviews
- Kevin posts on all channels
- Paperback goes live same day (usually — KDP Paperback can lag 1-3 days for review)

### Week 1-2: promote and watch metrics

- Amazon Ads (AMS) start at $5-10/day automatic campaign
- Monitor BSR (Best Sellers Rank), conversion rate from page to buy
- Adjust keywords if ranking weak

### Week 3-4: stabilize and decide

- Ebook price goes to $6.99 (standard)
- KDP Select cycle starts counting for 90 days
- Decide in week 12 whether to renew KDP Select or go wide

## Step 8: Direct sale on Kevin's Shopify (the B-path lead-magnet integration)

### Setup

1. **Product page**: "The [Book Title] — PDF edition, $19.99" or $29.99
2. **Shopify Digital Downloads app** (free, by Shopify) for delivery
3. **Klaviyo flow**: on-purchase → send PDF via email + bonus content (spreadsheet templates, video walkthrough, etc.) → 7-day post-purchase email asking for Amazon review → 30-day "what did you think" email asking for testimonial
4. **Upsells on Shopify product page**:
   - "Book + 1-hour consult" tier (higher price, higher margin)
   - "Book + template bundle" tier
   - "Paperback (signed copy mailed from SG)" tier

### Lead-magnet integration (the B-purpose)

Even if the book is primarily a paid product, Kevin can use **chapter 1 as a lead magnet**:

1. Chapter 1 extracted as a standalone PDF (12-20 pages)
2. Opt-in form on Kevin's Shopify store: "Read chapter 1 free"
3. Klaviyo flow: email 1 delivers the chapter, email 2 pitches the full book, email 3-7 provides additional value, email 8 pitches the book again with a discount code
4. Tags these leads as "book-interest" segment, useful for product launches too

This ties directly into `dropship-email-sms` (the flow) and `dropship-brand-transition` (the book itself is brand-equity building).

## Step 9: IngramSpark (for bookstore distribution)

If Kevin wants the book in independent bookstores or libraries:

- IngramSpark setup fee: $49 for ebook, $49 for paperback (total $98)
- Pro: Distribution to bookstores, libraries, international physical retail
- Con: Returns policy (unsold books can be returned at Kevin's cost) — turn OFF returns for first book to limit risk

Recommendation: **SKIP IngramSpark for first book.** KDP Print has expanded distribution that covers 80% of the benefit for zero extra fees. Revisit for book 2 if Kevin wants to push into indie bookstores specifically.

## Step 10: Translate to other languages

Only after the English book has sold 500+ copies and earned 50+ reviews. Order of priority:

1. **German** (large, engaged business book market, high willingness to pay)
2. **Spanish** (volume, Latin America especially)
3. **Japanese** (niche but premium pricing, longer runway)

Use services: Reedsy translator marketplace, TranslatorsCafe, Upwork vetted. Cost: $0.08-$0.15/word, so $3200-$6000 for a 40k-word book. Do NOT use machine translation for authority books — readers detect and reviews punish.

## Anti-patterns to refuse

- **Review swaps / review farms / paid reviews.** Refuse. Tell Kevin the math: one review purge + account ban costs more than 500 legitimate reviews would have earned.
- **Trademark-infringing keywords.** Refuse. Using "Shopify Academy" or a competitor author's name is a takedown waiting to happen.
- **Launching without paperback proof in hand.** Refuse.
- **Launching without A+ Content.** It is free and lifts conversion. No reason to skip.
- **Skipping Author Central.** It is free and builds author-brand equity across books. No reason to skip.
- **Going wide on launch week.** Refuse. Concentrate on one platform for launch velocity, then diversify.

## Tax and legal callouts for Kevin specifically

- **W-8BEN-E** must be completed during KDP tax interview, before first royalty. Amazon's default without it is 30% US withholding.
- **Singapore-US tax treaty** gives 5% withholding on book royalties (verify current rate at IRAS or the treaty text).
- **Royalty income in SG**: sourced where? If the IP was developed in Singapore, generally Singapore-sourced. Route to `dropship-legal-compliance` for proper treatment.
- **GST on digital goods sold to SG customers**: if Kevin's Pte Ltd is GST-registered, Overseas Vendor Registration rules apply. If selling direct PDF on his Shopify store, he needs to charge GST to SG buyers.
- **Depreciation/expense**: cover design, editor, ISBN fees, proof copies, software subscriptions — all deductible as business expenses of the Pte Ltd.

Route tax questions to `dropship-legal-compliance` and `dropship-cashflow-ops`. Do not attempt to answer them in this skill.
